02 The operating problem

The risky moment
is reuse.

A digital replica may be properly captured and still become hard to govern when it is adapted, transferred, or reused. Each handoff can separate the asset from the authority, restrictions, and evidence that made the original use acceptable.

A / 01
⌁

Context changes

A new medium, territory, edit, vendor, or production can exceed the original approval.

Risk: scope ambiguity
A / 02
◎

Evidence fragments

Contracts, approvals, asset records, and delivery instructions live in different systems.

Risk: slow, inconsistent review
A / 03
↻

Assets keep moving

Production partners need clear, current instructions without taking on unnecessary biometric custody.

Risk: uncontrolled handoff

03 The focused thesis

Coordinate the authorization packet.
Do not become the asset vault.

Perpetuity AI is designed as an operational layer between business affairs, rights holders, production teams, and approved vendors. The initial product should reference protected assets held by trusted custodians while organizing the human decisions and evidence around their use.

INITIAL POSITIONING Authorization operations Not generation · not a marketplace

04 The proposed product

One project-specific
authorization packet.

The packet assembles the information a production needs to make, communicate, and later reconstruct a decision. It is evidence for review — not a substitute for counsel or a guarantee that a use is lawful.

06 Trust posture

Earn trust before
claiming scale.

Digital replicas can expose biometric, contractual, creative, and reputational interests. The launch posture therefore minimizes custody, keeps decisions human, and labels every claim by what the evidence can actually support.

01

Specific by default

Every request is tied to a named project, purpose, boundary, and decision path.

02

Minimal custody

Reference protected assets held by approved custodians instead of centralizing raw biometric payloads.

03

Human authority

Software organizes evidence and routing; accountable people make approval and compensation decisions.

04

Least privilege

Access should be limited by role, project, purpose, and time, with consequential actions attributable.

05

Honest provenance

Tamper-evident records can show history; they do not prove that an underlying legal claim is true.

06

Evidence over theater

No security certification, legal clearance, or market adoption is claimed before independent proof exists.

07 Research integrity

Every important claim
gets a status.

Public facts, product observations, design inferences, and untested hypotheses are deliberately separated. Sources are dated, first-party claims are labeled, and changing law is never treated as static product logic.

SUPPORTED

Primary evidence

A dated official source supports the statement and is linked for independent review.

OBSERVED

Market observation

A vendor or public artifact demonstrates that an offering or behavior exists, not that it works as claimed.

HYPOTHESIS

Needs validation

A product, buyer, pricing, or outcome assumption remains explicitly unproven until a pilot generates evidence.
