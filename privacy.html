Privacy.

Effective 7 October 2026

This notice describes the limited data practices of this public preview website. It does not describe a future Perpetuity AI product or a customer pilot, which would require separate, context-specific terms and notices.

Data this site collects

This site does not use account registration, advertising pixels, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or embedded contact forms. It does not intentionally collect biometric information or upload digital-replica assets.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider and network intermediaries may process standard technical information needed to deliver the site, such as IP address, request time, requested page, browser information, and security events. The site operator has not added an analytics service or request-log collection through the website code.

Email links

If you use an email link, your email application and providers process the message under their own terms. Do not send biometric files, confidential contracts, credentials, sensitive personal data, or production assets in an initial inquiry.

External links

The evidence room links to external official and first-party sources. Those sites operate under their own privacy practices. Following a link may allow the destination to receive normal request information.

Children

This business-to-business preview is not directed to children and does not knowingly solicit personal information from them.

Changes

This notice will be revised before any product, account, form, analytics, or pilot-data collection is introduced. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this website notice may be sent through the privacy contact email. Please do not include sensitive information in the first message.
