# Security policy

Perpetuity AI is currently a static public preview and pilot-stage operating concept. There is no production customer application or bug-bounty program.

## Reporting

Send a concise report to `dan@dantrezise.com` with the subject `Perpetuity AI security report`.

Include:

- affected page or component;
- reproduction steps;
- potential impact; and
- any safe proof needed to understand the issue.

Do not include credentials, biometric files, confidential contracts, production assets, or unrelated personal data. Do not perform denial-of-service testing, destructive testing, social engineering, or access to data that does not belong to you.

Good-faith reports will be acknowledged when received. Response and remediation timing will depend on severity and the current project stage.
