# Bottom-up financial model framework

Status: template; no forecast is approved  
Last updated: 2026-10-07

## Rule

Do not reuse legacy capital, revenue, or operating-cost ranges. Build every number from a named driver, dated source, quote, contract, or observed delivery actual.

## Commercial stages

### Paid discovery

`revenue = signed discovery engagements × fee per engagement`

Use a fixed scope: baseline map, risk register, packet design, and pilot protocol.

### Concierge pilot

`pilot revenue = signed pilots × fixed pilot fee + approved pass-through costs`

`pilot contribution = pilot revenue - direct labor - specialist review - approved tools - direct travel - other delivery cost`

Track founder labor even when unpaid. A business model that works only by excluding founder delivery time is not validated.

### Repeat enterprise service

`annual account revenue = platform or retainer fee + transaction volume × transaction fee + premium services`

Do not select this model until repeat pilots reveal which cost and value driver buyers recognize.

## Required inputs

| Input | Evidence class |
| --- | --- |
| Sales conversion and cycle | Observed pipeline and signed agreements |
| Pilot price | Signed statement of work |
| Delivery hours by role | Time records |
| Legal and specialist cost | Engagement letter or quote |
| Software and infrastructure | Vendor invoice or current quote |
| Insurance | Broker quote |
| Security and assurance | Scoped provider quote |
| Support cost | Actual ticket and labor history |
| Transaction volume | Customer process data |
| Payment timing | Contract terms and actual collection |
| Churn or renewal | Actual repeat decision |

## Scenario design

Maintain three driver-based cases:

- downside: slower sales, lower price, higher delivery effort, no repeat;
- base: only evidence-backed assumptions;
- upside: constrained by demonstrated capacity and buyer commitments.

Each assumption needs an owner, source date, confidence, and sensitivity range.

## Pilot dashboard

- contracted revenue;
- collected cash;
- direct delivery hours and cost;
- contribution margin before founder normalization;
- contribution margin after founder normalization;
- days from first meeting to signature;
- days from invoice to cash;
- repeat intent and next committed transaction;
- implementation effort expected on the next pilot.

## Capital gate

Do not raise against speculative studio, capture, vault, or marketplace costs. Define capital needs only after the first paid pilot establishes the product boundary, risk posture, delivery cost, buyer cycle, and most likely path to repeat revenue.
