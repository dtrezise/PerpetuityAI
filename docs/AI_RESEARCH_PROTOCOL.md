# AI-assisted research and integrity protocol

Status: operating standard  
Last updated: 2026-10-07

## Purpose

Use current high-capability models to increase research breadth, synthesis quality, adversarial review, and artifact quality without allowing model fluency to substitute for evidence.

## Capability-based routing

Select models and tools by task, not prestige:

- fast model for classification, formatting, extraction, and routine transforms;
- frontier reasoning model for ambiguous strategy, cross-domain synthesis, and red-team review;
- deep-research workflow for current external evidence requiring traceable sources;
- structured outputs for claim ledgers, interview coding, risk registers, and evaluation data;
- browser or computer tools for visual and interactive verification;
- human expert review for legal conclusions, security assurance, financial commitments, and high-impact rights decisions.

Record the planned route and the model that actually executed consequential work. Never imply a recommended model ran when host metadata shows otherwise.

## Research workflow

1. Write the decision question, audience, date boundary, and acceptable evidence.
2. Search primary and authoritative sources first.
3. Separate law, proposed law, collective agreement, standard, first-party vendor claim, independent evidence, and opinion.
4. Capture source title, issuer, URL, publication/effective date, retrieval date, jurisdiction, and relevant proposition.
5. Add every consequential statement to the claim register.
6. Run a contradiction pass and seek disconfirming evidence.
7. Use a separate review role to test scope, freshness, inference, and citation fit.
8. Freeze the source register with the deliverable.
9. State unavailable materials and confidence limits.
10. Recheck changing authority before external reliance.

## Standard expert roles

- Research director: frames questions and source hierarchy.
- Evidence editor: verifies citation-to-claim fit and dates.
- Legal issue spotter: identifies questions requiring qualified counsel.
- Product strategist: converts evidence into falsifiable product hypotheses.
- Security and privacy architect: tests data boundaries and failure modes.
- Financial reviewer: rebuilds estimates from drivers, quotes, and actuals.
- Red team: searches for competition, conflicts, incentives, and reasons to stop.
- Publication editor: checks clarity, accessibility, visual hierarchy, and disclosure.

One person or model may perform multiple roles, but each review lens must produce an explicit result.

## Claim standard

Each claim receives:

- unique identifier;
- exact statement;
- type: verified fact, artifact observation, inference, recommendation, hypothesis, or superseded legacy claim;
- source or test;
- scope and as-of date;
- confidence;
- owner;
- next validation action; and
- external-use status.

## Model integrity rules

- Never invent sources, interviews, customers, quotes, metrics, or legal status.
- Open the cited source and verify that it supports the nearby claim.
- Treat vendor pages as evidence of public positioning, not independent proof of outcomes.
- Do not merge jurisdictions or agreements into a universal rule.
- Do not report generated financial ranges without drivers and source classes.
- Preserve disagreements and missing evidence.
- Use excerpts sparingly; prefer accurate paraphrase.
- Require human approval for external claims and high-impact decisions.

## Evaluations

Maintain a small test set for recurring tasks:

- correct source classification;
- citation supports claim;
- current versus proposed law;
- omission of sensitive data;
- detection of overbroad authorization;
- detection of material context change;
- correct handling of uncertainty;
- accessible, non-deceptive public copy.

Score model and workflow changes against the same set before adoption.

## Confidentiality

Do not place confidential contracts, unreleased assets, biometric data, credentials, or personal information into an AI environment unless the buyer has approved the service, configuration, retention, access, and contractual terms for that data class.
