02 Claim method

Four labels, one discipline.

VERIFIED FACT

Primary support

A dated primary or authoritative source directly supports the statement.

OBSERVATION

Directly visible

A project artifact or first-party vendor page shows what exists or is claimed.

INFERENCE

An analytical conclusion follows from evidence but is not itself independently proven.

HYPOTHESIS

Test required

A buyer, product, price, or outcome assumption remains open until measured.

03 Current findings

What the evidence supports.

VERIFIED FACT

Digital-replica obligations are increasingly specific.

Current union agreements and state statutes address consent, permitted use, secure handling, transfer, and related obligations in different contexts. Product logic must preserve the applicable agreement and jurisdiction rather than flattening them into one universal rule.

SAG-AFTRA 2026 TV/Theatrical resources ↗
VERIFIED FACT

Federal policy remains a moving target.

The U.S. Copyright Office has published its digital-replicas analysis, while proposed federal legislation can change during the legislative process. Current status must be verified at the time of any decision.

U.S. Copyright Office AI initiative ↗
DESIGN INFERENCE

Provenance integrity is not the same as legal authority.

C2PA can support tamper-evident provenance assertions. Perpetuity’s workflow must still identify who made an authority claim, the evidence and agreement behind it, its scope, and its expiry or conflict path.

C2PA specification 2.4 ↗
MARKET OBSERVATION

The category is active and increasingly crowded.

Agency vaults, licensing marketplaces, provenance systems, enforcement vendors, and rights-infrastructure companies already address parts of the problem. The initial wedge must win inside a specific production workflow, not rely on category novelty.

CORE HYPOTHESIS

A production-side buyer will pay for authorization operations.

This remains unproven. The proposed pilot tests whether a structured packet and handoff process reduce measurable friction without requiring raw biometric custody.
