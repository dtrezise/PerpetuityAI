01 Pilot offer

A real workflow.
A measurable decision.

A proposed 12-week, paid, concierge pilot for one production-side digital-replica reuse or transfer. The engagement creates a reviewable authorization packet, operates the handoff, and measures whether the process is worth productizing.

02 Entry criteria

Narrow enough to learn.

GOOD PILOT FIT
  • A real project and accountable production-side sponsor
  • An existing or anticipated digital likeness, voice, or performance asset
  • A reuse, adaptation, transfer, or vendor handoff that requires human approval
  • Access to business affairs, production, rights-holder, and vendor participants
  • Willingness to establish a baseline and share redacted outcome evidence
NOT THE FIRST PILOT
  • A request for a talent marketplace or speculative asset catalog
  • A need for automated legal conclusions or universal identity verification
  • A requirement that the pilot hold raw biometric files without a validated reason
  • A procurement exercise without a live workflow or empowered sponsor
  • A promise of compliance certification before controls are independently assessed

03 Engagement phases

Twelve weeks to evidence.

  1. WEEKS 1–2Baseline

    Map the transaction, roles, systems, documents, review time, missing information, exceptions, and failure modes.

  2. WEEKS 3–4Define

    Agree on the authorization packet, data boundaries, human approvals, vendor instructions, measures, and stop conditions.

  3. WEEKS 5–9Operate

    Run the workflow with minimal-custody tools, maintain a decision log, and document every material exception.

  4. WEEKS 10–12Close out

    Confirm disposition, assemble the evidence record, compare outcomes with baseline, and make a build, revise, or stop decision.

04 Deliverables

An operating result, not a pitch deck.

01

Current-state map

People, systems, source documents, decision rights, delays, and failure modes.

02

Authorization packet

Structured request, authority basis, bounded terms, approvals, asset references, and instructions.

03

Handoff protocol

Role-specific delivery, acknowledgements, exception handling, retention, and closeout requirements.

04

Outcome report

Measured baseline versus result, unresolved risks, stakeholder feedback, and product recommendation.

05 Decision measures

What success must prove.

MeasureQuestion
Cycle timeDid the request move from intake to decision faster than the baseline?
CompletenessDid reviewers receive the material information they needed the first time?
ReworkWere fewer clarification loops, duplicate entries, or corrective handoffs required?
Evidence qualityCould an authorized reviewer reconstruct the decision and production handoff?
Buyer valueWill the sponsor pay to repeat the workflow without founder-led intervention?
Risk boundaryCan value be delivered without central custody of raw biometric assets?

06 Pilot conversation

Bring one difficult handoff.

The first conversation is a fit check, not a sales commitment. Please describe the project, participating roles, current process, digital-replica use, and the decision or handoff that creates friction.
